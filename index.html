About

Despite his young age and ongoing studies at the Jazz Department in Trondheim, Nikolai Hansli has already taken part in numerous musical collaborations, including projects with many prominent names in the music scene. Along the way, he has received several accolades, including the Bærum Artist Grant and the Bærum Musician Award, the latter also leading to a commissioned work for Bærum Jazz Festival.

As well as playing with different projects, Nikolai Hansli currently has a residency at Levant in Oslo, where he hosts and serves as musical director every weekend, in a cooperation with Jazz Music Norway. In addition to that, Hansli also has a monthly arrangement called «salmekveld» in Hoeggen Church in Trondheim. The concept evolves around meditative evenings where people gather to sing together, as well as listening to musical interpretations of hymns.

      Selected projects

      Hansli’s Endurance

      Hansli’s Endurance brings together four individual voices that push the music forward into improvised passages that have never been played before. The band focuses on an overflowing energy that carries the phrases and shapes the music.

      The sound combines hard bop, gospel, blues, meditative music, and the energetic spirit of the avant-garde and the great John Coltrane.

      They strive for a rawness in the music, an energy that at times can become gritty and serious, but that can also create a sense of calm, hope, and meditative reflection.

      The band released their first album the summer of 2026, while the second album is expected in 2027.

      Nikolai Hansli: Nye Røtter

      This project brings together both familiar and lesser-known traditional Norwegian folk texts and songs, weaving them into newly composed original music.

      With humility and curiosity, the band seeks to create a thread of connection and dialogue between ancient traditions and a fresh, personal interpretation.

      Drawing inspiration from a wide range of musical worlds, including blues, jazz, gospel, sacred music, meditative music, and folk traditions themselves, the band creates a unique sonic landscape and an atmosphere that invites interactive spontaneity.

      The project was originally commissioned by Bærum Jazz Festival, and the band’s debut album is expected in 2027.

      Nikolai Hansli Quartet

      This project is the oldest band out of all the active Nikolai Hansli bands. The quartet is a Norwegian/Danish jazz quartet, playing original music. The band conveys lyrical music, and tries to unite melancholy and seriousness with hope and devotion.

      In 2025, the band went on a successful tour, and there is more to come in 2027.
